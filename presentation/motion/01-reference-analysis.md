# 01 · Reference analysis

**Source:** `careerhive-ui/src/assets/motion.mp4`, a third-party motion-design reel (watermark `@alee.gfx_`, end card
"Web Rank Studio"). It is 19.1 s long, 720 × 1280 (9:16), 30 fps, with a voice-over over a music bed.

It was studied frame by frame (3 fps contact sheets, 10 fps strips around every cut, scene-change detection,
waveform and silence detection). It is used as a **benchmark for motion quality and pacing only**. No frame, object,
typeface or colour from it is reused, and the file itself should not be committed or published (see
[05-assets.md](05-assets.md)).

## What the reference teaches (principles)

1. **One idea per beat.** 11 scenes in 19 s (1.7 s on average). The longest shot (3.6 s) is the one that explains.
2. **A lead-in and a keyword.** Every line pairs a small, quiet phrase with one huge word. Hierarchy comes from size and
   weight, not from colour.
3. **One accent colour.** Purple, white, cream and black only. Colour flips are punctuation.
4. **The focus pull is the signature entrance.** Things arrive blurred and slightly large, then settle sharp.
5. **Transitions come out of the shot.** The iris grows from the TV, the floor rises under the column, the card flies
   off the frame. Nothing is a generic preset.
6. **Silence is a tool.** Three short silences (4.1, 14.7, 16.0 s) reset attention before a new idea.
7. **Echo typography.** Giant pale copies of the words add depth without adding information.
8. **Restraint.** No glitch, no shake, no particles. The only "effects" are blur, motion blur and a few shards.

## What CareerHive does differently (on purpose)

| Reference | CareerHive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| stock objects as metaphors (brain, eye, TV, chess king) | **the real product** is the object: captured screens, rings, charts, cards | the film has to prove the product exists and works |
| italic serif + condensed caps | the product's fonts: **Bricolage Grotesque 800**, Onest, Martian Mono | the film must look like the app it advertises |
| purple accent | **Frost** blue (`#2F7BF6`, gradient `#6CC4FF → #2F7BF6 → #1C3FC9`); Ember red only for the problem; Night navy for technical scenes | the app's own themes |
| circle iris, shards | **hexagon** iris, honeycomb, corner hexagons | the *Hive* in CareerHive, an original motif |
| voice-over carries the story | a voice-over **and** on-screen kinetic type carry it, with the problem words and "Learn. Prove. Grow." spoken as they land ([03-storyboards.md](03-storyboards.md)) | the film works with sound and muted, as most feeds play it |
| 1.7 s average beat | 4.8 s feature beats (2 bars) in 16:9, 2.4 s (1 bar) in 9:16 | real UI needs reading time; the vertical cut keeps the reference's pace |
