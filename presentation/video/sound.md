# Music & sound design

**Music:** minimal modern electronic / "corporate tech" with warmth — soft synth pads, plucked arps, light percussion,
a clear build at 0:06 and a resolve at 0:45. **Avoid:** epic trailer drums, dubstep drops, vocals.
**BPM:** 100–118 (cut on the beat: at 110 BPM a beat is ~0.55 s, so the 3 s shots are ~5.5 beats).

| Sound | Where | Character | Search keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro riser | 0:00–0:03 | soft, filtered, 2–3 s | "soft riser cinematic", "airy swell" |
| Impact | wordmark 0:03, end card 0:47 | low, rounded, no crash | "soft impact low", "sub hit clean" |
| UI clicks | every on-screen click | short, quiet, mid-high | "ui click soft", "interface tap subtle" |
| Notification pop | bell 0:21, like 0:30 | friendly blip (the app has its own two-tone pop you can record) | "notification pop soft", "bubble pop ui" |
| Whoosh | problem → solution, into the architecture | airy, short (≤ 0.5 s) | "whoosh short airy", "swish transition soft" |
| Keyboard ticks | team hub | very low in the mix | "keyboard typing soft close" |
| Ambient bed | under the whole film, −30 dB | room tone / light air | "room tone office quiet", "ambient air tone" |

**Royalty-free sources (check each licence when downloading):**
- **YouTube Audio Library:** free; some tracks require attribution in the description.
- **Pixabay Music and Pixabay Sound Effects:** free under the Pixabay licence.
- **Uppbeat:** free tier with a credit line; paid removes the credit.
- **Mixkit:** free music and SFX under the Mixkit licence.
- **freesound.org:** per-file licences (CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-NC). Use CC0 or CC-BY with credit; avoid NC for anything commercial.
- **Paid (simplest licensing for social and portfolio):** Artlist, Epidemic Sound, Musicbed.

Music search keywords: "minimal tech corporate", "modern technology inspiring", "clean electronic product",
"future bass light 110 bpm", "positive tech startup".

**Licensing:** don't use commercial music you don't have a licence for; LinkedIn and Instagram may mute or block it.
Keep a `licences/` folder with the download receipts and licence text for every track and sound.
