# CareerHive — product film · storyboard

**Length:** 50 s master (a 30 s cut is marked below) · **Format:** 16:9 1920×1080 master, plus 9:16 and 1:1 re-frames
**Rendered:** `CareerHive-Film-1080p.mp4` follows this storyboard; its features beat shows team hub · feed · **CV coach** (analysing → results) · skills · themes.
**Idea:** *"Grow on purpose."* The path motif (Learn → Prove → Grow) draws itself, and real screens show each step. The
product is the hero; stock footage is optional texture and never the subject. Every claim on screen is a real feature.

**IP note:** the Rick mascot and the Rick-and-Morty emblem are fan-made models of copyrighted characters. For a public
release, prefer shots where they're small or absent (formations, reviews, feed, org panel, team hub). The sign-in
portal entrance is striking, but keep it for internal or academic viewing unless you have the rights.
